SLA rules used on this page
  • Goal: no open FI older than 14 days, counted from Fulfillment Item: Created Date.
  • Consultation: aging when created more than 14 days ago.
  • Launch: any Launch FI still In Progress is aging. It should have Domain/URL, Client is currently using DBE, and Login Information Acquired filled in, then be completed.
  • Review: aging when FI is 14+ days old AND (Design Review is blank or the date has passed).
  • Unengaged: aging when FI is 14+ days old AND has been in Unengaged more than 5 business days (from Function Aging Days).
  • Upcoming scheduled review: FI is 14+ days old AND Design Review date is today or later, in any tracked function. Its own box shows the count; these FIs are also included in Total tracked and All tracked FIs, but not in the function boxes.
  • Only Consultation, Launch, Review and Unengaged FIs are included.
